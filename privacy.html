Dark Grid - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Dark Grid is a free, ad-supported puzzle game. This policy explains what data is involved when you play.

1. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal data. Your high score, saved game and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Your choices

• On iOS you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

• Where required by law, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change this choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy options inside the game.

• If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are not personalized.

4. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete. Deleting the app removes all game data from your device.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
